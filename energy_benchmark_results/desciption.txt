Benchmarking molecular conformer ensemble generation should answer 3 questions:
1. Is the model spanning conformational space?
2. Is the model producing reasonable structures?
3. Is the model producing the lowest energy structure?

CovMat score answers the first question. The energy-based benchmark seeks to answer the next two. We answer question 2 by taking the average energy difference between the optimized and non-optimized generated structures. This tells us how close to a local minimum a model generates structures from the ensemble. This is a way to define reasonableness. We answer question 3 by comparing the lowest energy generated structure after it's been optimized to the lowest energy ground truth structure. This tells us how well we find the global minimum.

This data contains the energy of all generated structures for each method before and after optimization and the energy of the ground truth structures for 20 molecular ensembles from GEOMXL. There is frequency data for some structures for validating how well the optimization went. You can ignore it, I decided frequency calculations for all structures would be too slow. Some optimizations failed but very few, those are not included here. I optimized all of the ground truth structures too, but realized they were already optimized so the pre and post optimization numbers should be the same.

To run this benchmark you must:
1. Compute the average energy difference between pre and post optimized structures for each ensemble for each generation method, and a cumulative average across all ensembles for a given method.
2. Compute the difference between the lowest energy generated structure after optimization and the lowest energy ground truth structure for each method.
